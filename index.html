Who he is

Augustus Manowar answers to Oggie and has since basic. Human, mid-thirties, built like a man who spent a decade carrying other men. He was a company medic in the fighting at Elturel — not a soldier who learned first aid, a medic who learned soldiering because the alternative was watching.

When Elturel was pulled down into Avernus, Oggie worked the streets under a burning sky for as long as the city hung there, dragging the wounded out of collapsed houses while devils walked past him picking souls off the dying. He got a great many people home. He is exact about the number he didn't.

He mustered out the season after the city came back and went looking for somewhere quiet. He found Ilmater, the Crying God, and a house of his monks who taught the Way of Mercy. He came for the healing. What he found was that the discipline has two hands: one that closes a wound and one that opens one, trained by the same drills, thrown with the same body. Nobody at the monastery pretended otherwise. That honesty is the reason he stayed.

Now he walks. He does not go looking for a fight. But some fights need having, and he has two legs, two fists, two knees, and two elbows ready when one does.
